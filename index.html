1. Overview

Skillra AI is a learning application designed to support education and practice in medical coding and related life-science topics.

In this policy, "Skillra AI", "we", "us" and "our" refer to Skillra Health Innovations Pvt Ltd.

This policy applies to information processed through the Skillra AI mobile application and related services that link to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

The information we process depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

  • Account information: Name, email address, authentication identifiers and sign-in method.
  • Authentication: Skillra AI uses Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.
  • Learning profile: Education, specialization, experience level, medical coding experience, learning goals, target certification and daily learning goals.
  • Learning activity: Lessons viewed, quiz responses, scores, course progress, streaks and rewards.
  • AI interactions: Questions, prompts, messages and feedback submitted to AI mentors.
  • Uploaded content: Text, documents, images, audio or other materials you choose to submit through available app features.
  • Technical information: App version, device type, operating system, timestamps, crash reports and diagnostic logs, where collected.

The app should request access to device features such as the camera, microphone, photos or files only when required by a feature.

We do not intend for users to submit real patient records, identifiable patient information or confidential clinical data.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users through supported sign-in methods.
  • Personalize lessons and AI mentor responses.
  • Save learning progress, scores and achievements.
  • Operate and improve the application.
  • Detect abuse and protect account security.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Skillra AI is an educational application and does not provide medical diagnoses or medical advice.

4. AI Learning Features

When you use AI mentor, chat, explanation or other AI-powered features, the information you submit may be sent to the AI service provider configured for that feature.

This may include your prompts, conversation context and relevant learning information required to generate a response.

Before publication, we will identify the actual AI providers used in production, their data processing practices, retention policies and model-training settings.

AI-generated responses may be incomplete or inaccurate. Users should independently verify educational information.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the application.

These may include:

  • Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.
  • Database and hosting infrastructure providers.
  • AI and voice processing providers.
  • Analytics and diagnostic services, if enabled.
  • Customer support and communication providers.

The final published policy will identify the actual production services that receive user data and link to their relevant privacy policies.

6. Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

These measures may include access controls, secure connections and safeguards provided by our infrastructure vendors.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Information may be processed or stored in countries other than your country of residence, depending on our service providers.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain learning records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

Actual retention periods for account data, learning records, AI conversations, uploaded content, logs and backups must be confirmed before publication.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can review and update certain profile information within the application where those controls are available.

You can also manage device permissions through your operating system settings.

Account and Data Deletion

Account deletion is not implemented yet. Skillra AI does not currently provide a completed in-app account deletion flow or a dedicated web-based account deletion request flow.

If users can create accounts in the released app, the required account deletion functionality must be implemented in accordance with applicable Google Play requirements.

This includes an accessible in-app deletion option and a public web resource through which users can request deletion.

Once implemented, this section will explain the request process, verification, deletion timeline and any legally required retention.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing.

For privacy questions, contact: admin@skillra.com
